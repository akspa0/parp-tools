# Contract: Reversible Renderer Optimization Gate

Every candidate optimization must provide:

- one selected owner;
- one runtime or capture switch, or a clearly isolated fallback boundary;
- before/after reports from the same control scene;
- changed workload counters and stage timing;
- fallback counts/reasons for unsupported or sensitive content;
- visual-gate status;
- accepted/rejected/deferred decision.

The candidate is accepted only when:

- the selected owner improves by at least 15 percent or 5 ms at the control scene;
- total p95 CPU frame time does not regress;
- no visible terrain, hole, liquid, WMO, M2/MDX, or material-sensitive class disappears without an
  explained and approved behavior change;
- the old path remains selectable until user-owned real-client proof is complete.

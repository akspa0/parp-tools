# Phase 3 — MSHD word analysis (2026-10-20)

## Question

The parser was brute-forced without fully analyzing MSHD. What do its eight words carry?

## Method

[`Pm4MshdProbe`](../../../src/core/WowViewer.Core.PM4/Research/Pm4MshdProbe.cs) records per-file
word statistics and Pearson correlations against file-level counts and tile coordinates. Correlation
is not semantics.

## Conclusion

Five of eight words (0x0C, 0x10, 0x14, 0x18, 0x1C) are **always zero** in this corpus. Field00 and
Field08 are near-identical (median 512/509, max 534) and correlate with the destructible-building
count (Mdbh) at ~0.76–0.79; Field04 is smaller and more variable. This is consistent with a
header/region descriptor, but the exact meaning remains **U**. Source: **E-MSHDP**.

# parp-tools WoW Viewer v0.6.0-alpha4

A major performance, terrain accuracy, and rendering release for **modern CASC clients and historical WoW data**.
This build performs significantly better than v0.6.0-alpha3, introduces full 64-bit high-resolution terrain hole
support across modern ADT maps (fixing cave entrances, dungeon thresholds, and raycast occlusion), adds an M2 opacity
slider tied to WMO/M2/MDX wireframes, enables custom wireframe colors and styling in settings, fixes particle light
effects opacity, enables precise scene cursor object picking, and adds community acknowledgment for Kruithne's `wow.export`.

---

## 1. Modern ADT 64-Bit High-Resolution Terrain Holes

- **High-Res Hole Decoding**: Modern ADTs (MoP 5.3+ through modern Retail/WoW: Forever) carry an 8-byte 64-bit hole mask
  at MCNK offset `0x14` flagged by bit 16 (`0x10000`). The viewer now parses both 64-bit high-res masks and legacy 16-bit
  masks seamlessly across all mesh builders (`TerrainTileMeshBuilder`, `TerrainMeshBuilder`, `MapGlbExporter`,
  `TerrainHeightmapIo`, and `TerrainChunkMath`).
- **Cave & Underground Opening Fix**: Caves, basements, and dungeon entrances no longer render with solid phantom terrain
  over their thresholds.
- **Accurate Raycasting & Object Picking**: Height sampling (`TerrainQueryService`, `SceneTerrainQueries`) now respects
  terrain holes, allowing raycasts, camera navigation, and mouse clicks to pass cleanly through cave openings directly
  into underlying WMO buildings and doodad models.

## 2. Rendering & Interaction Enhancements

- **M2 Opacity Control**: Added opacity slider for M2 models with proper shader and renderer integration.
- **Unified Wireframe Overlay**: WMO, M2, and MDX wireframe overlay intensities are now synchronized with the opacity
  slider, preventing blinding or overwhelming wireframe intensity.
- **Wireframe Customization**: New options in the Settings panel allow users to customize wireframe overlay color, alpha,
  and rendering design.
- **Particle & Light Blending**: Particle effects with lights opacity calculation corrected across multi-layer visual effects.
- **3D Scene Cursor**: Enabled 3D cursor rendering in scene for pinpoint object identification and click selection.
- **Toolbar & Sidebar UI Polish**: Removed redundant and fragile doodad-set combo from the bottom toolbar in favor of the full-featured Inspector panel; eliminated duplicate and nested "MCNK Flag Overlay" menus in the right sidebar.

## 3. Performance & Modern Data Streaming

- **Massive Performance Boost**: Building on v0.6.0-alpha3, modern CASC streaming and frame submission have been deeply
  optimized for smoother framerates, faster chunk admission, and lower GC pressure.
- **Decomposed Architecture**: Cleaned architectural boundaries across scene queries and renderers in conformance with
  SpecKit governance.

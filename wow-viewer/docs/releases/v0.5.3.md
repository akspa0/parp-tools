# parp-tools WoW Viewer v0.5.3

Official release for **v0.5.3** — bringing over **180 commits** of new capability, architectural stability, and format rendering across Alpha 0.5.3 through Cataclysm 4.0.x and Mists of Pandaria 5.0.1 groundwork.

---

## 1. Legacy MDX & M2 Model Support & Animation Playback (Spec 235)

- **1.x M2 Bone Animation Playback**:
  - Restored full-body skeletal limb and movement animations on 1.x M2 models (e.g., 1.12.1).
  - Fixed header parsing in `M2Era1121ModelReader`: `+0x04` decoded as `Duration` and `+0x08` as `MoveSpeed` with zero baseline start timestamps, preventing keyframe time evaluation from overshooting the timeline and clamping bones to the final frame.
- **Tree Foliage & Alpha Cutout Discard Restoration**:
  - Resolved missing pine needle canopies and exposed trunk cones on trees (e.g. `wintertree01.mdx`).
  - Tuned `alphaCutoutThreshold` to `0.15f` to preserve antialiased needle edges and foliage mips across distance.
  - Corrected MDX texture wrapping and clamp bit decoding (`WrapWidth` 0x1, `WrapHeight` 0x2).
- **Two-Sided Surface Lighting**:
  - Added normal inversion on back-facing polygons in `M2Renderer` so two-sided leaves, branches, and needles receive proper diffuse illumination instead of turning pitch black.
- **Character Customization & Armor Extrusions**:
  - Pruned default 3D armor attachment geoset groups (`401, 501, 702, 801, 901, 1001`) on unarmored characters, preventing boots/sleeves/kneepants from extruding over underwear/skin.
  - Added race mapping for Goblin (`9`).
- **Sidebar Status Convergence**:
  - Replaced outdated prototype shading notes in the inspector with active runtime pipeline details.

---

## 2. Map Save & New Map Creator (Spec 234)

- **In-Viewer New Map Creator**:
  - Interactive UI dialog in the **Editor > Data I/O** tab (`NewMapCreatorService`).
  - Generate brand new LK ADT and WDT terrain maps directly from configurable parameters: Map Name, Theme (Lordaeron Summer, Barrens, Northrend Snow, Ashenvale, etc.), Grid Dimensions (2x2 up to 8x8), Plaza spacing, and Base Ground Elevation.
  - Automatically registers newly created maps as live viewer overlays and focuses the camera upon creation.
- **Merged ADT / Alpha WDT Export**:
  - Export composed terrain and placed objects into serialized game containers.

---

## 3. Cartography Composition & Cell-Level Layer Alignment (Spec 232)

- **Rigid Cell-Level Alignment**:
  - Overhauled sub-tile fine-tuning: donor maps and composed terrain layers now translate as rigid spatial units without slicing chunk geometry across arbitrary boundaries or scrambling vertex heights.
  - Placements, doodads, and WMOs cleanly track layer translation, rotation, and elevation offsets.
- **Project Persistence**:
  - Save and load multi-layer cartography projects with transform parameters, visibility toggles, and base-map channel gates preserved.
  - Protected locked layers against accidental deletion or clearing.
- **WDL Magnetic Edge-Snapping**:
  - Automatic alignment along WDL low-resolution terrain boundaries.

---

## 4. Editor Platform & UI Overhaul (Spec 231)

- **4-Page Editor IA**:
  - Consolidated workbench into four dedicated pages: **Terrain**, **Placement**, **Cartography**, and **Data I/O**.
  - Added persistent top toolbar controls including an `Anim` toggle for world doodads and hovered-WMO doodad set combos.
- **Surface Pruning & Deduplication**:
  - Removed 27 dead or redundant inspector surfaces, de-hosted obsolete Archaeology hosts, and centralized placement I/O.
- **Wireframe Rendering Refinement**:
  - Flat-color untextured line passes for terrain, M2 runtime, and MDX wireframe modes, fixing color washout and alpha clipping artifacts.

---

## 5. Rosetta: Procedural Map Generation & Calibration (Spec 190)

- **Deterministic Calibration Maps**:
  - `RosettaTilesetGenerator` for chunk-aligned, deterministic map asset placement, pedestal heightfields (`MCVT`), and ADT serialization.
  - High-resolution $1024 \times 1024$ MCAL/MCLY terrain labeling with antialiased quincunx sampling and 4-bit nibble slicing.
  - First-class Alpha 0.5.3 `Map.dbc` / `AreaTable.dbc` support.

## 6. PM4: Field Semantics & Reconciliation (Specs 176 & 188)

- **Behavior-Verified Semantics**:
  - Settled field mappings across real data: `MSUR._0x1C` as placement Z, `MSLK.LinkId` as reversed tile addresses, and `MSUR._0x00` surface classes.
  - Object placement regeneration from PM4 geometry for missing ADT tiles.
- **PM4-Guided Reconciliation**:
  - Real preview, residual-derived confidences, bulk accept, and staged-save integration for authored placement edits.

# parp-tools WoW Viewer v0.5.2

238 commits since `v0.5.1-build1`. This release rebuilds how the viewer decides what to draw —
portal-aware WMO visibility, a per-ADT scene graph, bounded camera-centered streaming — and adds a
working audio runtime, a real time-of-day clock, LIT lighting decode, and the completed PM4
coordinate solve.

## Highlights

- **WMO portal visibility** replaces the old center-distance heuristic (Spec 151).
- **Bounded, camera-centered terrain streaming** — nearby ADTs no longer pop out on camera turns.
- **Audio runtime** — positional MCSE/MCNK emitters, `SoundEntries` preview, master mute.
- **Alpha 0.5.3 time-of-day cycle** on the native 2,880-unit / 24-real-minute world clock.
- **PM4 coordinate frames solved and confirmed** — plus wall-mesh decode and per-doodad identity.
- **Workbench UI consolidation** into five destinations with contextual keybinds and shortcut help.

## World rendering and streaming (Specs 142, 150)

- Per-ADT independent scene graphs with conservative traversal, node-kind rejection attribution, and
  doodads partitioned by terrain chunk; opt-in and diagnostic-first.
- `DirectionalTileSelector` protects the largest complete camera-centered square the budget allows
  (3×3 for 9–24 tiles, 5×5 at 25) before spending remaining slots on the forward cone. Fixes nearby
  side/rear ADTs unloading while distant terrain stayed visible.
- `CameraTileWindowSelector` adds bounded retained residency — radius 2 default, radius 3 maximum —
  separate from the directional active list used for detailed terrain and objects.
- Tile and chunk distance admission now measures the nearest point on geometry bounds, not the
  center, so a camera near a tile edge keeps that tile.
- Resident neighbor tiles stay eligible for MDX/WMO collection even when outside the directional
  detail list; bounds and frustum tests remain the submission gate.
- Removed camera-heading-driven residency churn and rear-cone culling for already-admitted WMOs;
  added a capped unload-hysteresis ring. Active render tiles follow mouse-look without reopening the
  streaming lease.
- Pending GPU tile uploads are ranked by selected active tiles before retained and stale completions.
- GPU-instanced doodad rendering, batched static M2/WMO submissions, and a cross-WMO opaque
  internal-doodad path that groups shared renderers across visible placements.
- WDL horizon suppression follows the selected, GPU-resident detailed ADT set each frame; retained
  neighbors keep their WDL underlay until detailed terrain actually submits.
- Production render diagnostics: `ProductionWorldSceneProfiler`, stalled-phase exposure, scene
  maintenance cost attribution, and profiles anchored to Azeroth tile 32_32 within one client build.
- Full-map tiles are rejected before object traversal; normal terrain loads no longer spam the console.

## WMO portal visibility (Spec 151)

- Implements the 0.5.3 Ghidra-traced portal decision: exterior seeds, source-side plane admission,
  transformed portal polygons, recursively narrowed clip volumes, bounded depth/visit traversal.
- Fail-open by design — invalid geometry, singular placement transforms, boundary cameras, and
  capacity overflow stay conservative rather than dropping WMO surfaces.
- One decision now drives group geometry, doodad admission, and liquids. `WmoRenderStats` carries
  portal-tested, fallback, and admitted-group counters.
- Camera containment in *any* local group bounds counts as inside-WMO, even when root MOHD bounds
  miss the camera, so interior groups stay visible.
- Focused portal/graph tests pass 16/16. Real-client visual/submission/FPS comparison is user-owned.

## Lighting, sky, and time of day (Specs 106, 143)

- **Alpha 0.5.3 time-of-day cycle**: the 2,880-unit / 24-real-minute world clock is now a tested
  contract. Interactive lighting advances it from a monotonic frame clock; the manual slider freezes
  it until resumed; Light DBC/LIT/sky/audio all receive one same-frame value.
- **LIT decode**: pre-alpha v2 profile for the observed `areatest.lit` payload (embedded 64-byte
  Global Light header, 60-byte legacy prefix, two `0xA24` nine-track sets in a `0x1484` group).
  Modern v8.3–v8.5 track lengths stay strict `0..32`.
- **LIT spatial fix**: list-header positions decode client fixed-point XZY by dividing by 36 and
  swapping file Y/Z into semantic WoW XYZ. Positions were previously ~36× off-map.
- LIT discovery enumerates every `.lit` in the active map folder; variants can be switched even when
  the selected profile failed to parse. With no usable map-scoped Light DBC profile, LIT loads
  automatically and its lighting/fog override starts enabled.
- Client stars render during the night sky. Near terrain stays visible through fog admission.
- Synthetic minimap generation stays fixed at its requested time and records `timeOfDayMode=frozen`
  in its manifest.

## World context and area identity (Specs 143, 149)

- **Dual-era AreaTable routing**: Alpha 0.5.x uses packed MCNK `AreaNumber` high/low Zone/SubZone
  words with `ParentAreaNum`; 3.3.5+ uses direct `AreaTable.ID`/`ParentAreaID`. Modern direct IDs
  resolve through primary IDs only, so compatibility aliases can't steal a row that numerically
  resembles a packed Alpha `AreaNumber`.
- Removed the unsafe legacy aliases that treated the two packed component words as standalone IDs.
- Native area and subzone context in the status bar and overlay, resolved from batched terrain
  metadata.
- Opt-in resident Zone/Subzone footprint overlay: `AreaOverlayRegionBuilder` groups map-aware results
  into finite regions with deterministic colors and unresolved counts. Default off.

## Audio (Specs 146, 148, 149)

- Packaged OpenAL Soft for both viewer targets; the native probe retains the loaded module before
  Silk.NET constructs `AudioContext`. Missing OpenAL now fails closed and keeps the viewer alive.
- Build-aware standard and Alpha 0.5.3 MCSE emitter extraction. Corrected the Alpha record to its
  `0x34`-byte on-disk form (versus the client's `0x4c`-byte in-memory `CWSoundEmitter`) and preserved
  its scheduler fields.
- **Emitter coordinate fix**: the shared `TerrainCoordinateTransform` anchors renderer positions to
  the owning chunk before range checks, OpenAL placement, and diagnostics. Legacy MCNK liquid markers
  corrected from `chunk corner + halfChunk` to the terrain convention `chunk corner - halfChunk`.
- Resident MCNK flags/MCLQ/MH2O liquid state projects into typed environmental audio candidates;
  exact-build `SoundWaterType` rows resolve `(liquid family, subtype)` to `SoundEntries` IDs without
  inventing them. The Alpha MCLQ handoff now preserves 81 packed vertex records and 64 tile flags.
- Tools > Utilities > Audio: resident `SoundEntries` discovery, camera-local preview/stop, master and
  emitter gain, backend status, diagnostics.
- Off-by-default resident emitter markers — one source-colored 3D pin per finite resident emitter
  (amber MCSE, cyan MCNK water, purple MCNK environment).
- `AUDIO: ON` / `AUDIO: MUTED` status-bar button routed through the runtime master bus.
- Automatic ZoneMusic playback is **muted by policy** — resolution and diagnostics stay visible, but
  the indirection (`ZoneMusic` row → `Sounds[day/night]` → `SoundEntries`) is not yet proven.
  MIDI/DLS are reported as unsupported rather than guessed.

## Camera paths and capture (Spec 144)

- Cross-era camera import routes through `M2ModelReaderDispatcher`: explicit MD20 `0x109+` modern
  camera records with strict `0x74` span validation, plus the documented MD20 `0x100` early layout
  (`0x7c` records, `0x1c` tracks, range slicing). Removes the old unconditional camera suppression
  that caused `cameraIndex` failures.
- Swept-footprint path residency connects path samples in tile space and applies the configured tile
  radius, so fast/spline paths no longer skip ADTs that then unload. Path tile conversion corrected
  to ADT `TileSize` rather than terrain `ChunkSize`.
- Ordinary Play waits on the bounded preload lease when enabled; playback and capture completion
  release it. Capture paths warm before recording.
- Capture controls moved under Tools > Utilities > Capture; camera-path keyboard authoring is opt-in
  and scoped to the active Capture page.

## MDX and M2 model rendering (Spec 104)

- Restored the GLSL MDX material inputs the CPU path was already uploading: UV0/UV1 selection,
  animated UV transforms, view-normal sphere-environment mapping, plus a bounded `SphereEnvMap`
  reflective highlight and clamped model-local lighting/emissive values.
- Added classic `LITE` parsing to `MdxFile` (static Omni/Ambient, deferred `PIVT` resolution); the
  MDX shader now receives up to eight model-local light records.
- Implemented the premultiplied-alpha shader output the transparent MDX blend state already
  requested; preserved classic `MTLS` static emissive gain as self-illumination only.
- Removed white 1×1 fallbacks from transparent MDX geosets and unresolved particle emitters — missing
  effect textures now fail closed instead of drawing invented white squares.
- Contained MDX fragment-shader failures and unresolved-MDX streaming admission so a bad model can no
  longer block tile loading.
- Shared renderer-space placement transform for MDX and WMO, correcting the prior WMO-only
  positive-axis rotation path. Bounds use the same transform as mesh submission.

## Minimap and terrain overlays (Specs 135, 137, 147)

- Removed duplicate fullscreen minimap ownership; docked and fullscreen input route through one pure
  gesture state with exactly-once third-click teleport.
- Low-alpha fog-colored LIT radius fill and outline on minimap markers, colored from the active LIT
  fog track.
- Phased terrain dual-map overlay and phased minimap overlay; the Phase Map selector lives under
  Navigator > World Maps.
- PM4/PD4 version formatting in tool output.

## Synthesized minimaps and lighting corrections (Specs 111, 125)

- **Hillshade Y-axis inversion fixed** — image-space Y runs downward but the hillshade formula
  assumes upward Y, so a configured 315° azimuth rendered as south-west light.
- **Object capture Z backlighting fixed** — 0.5.3 geometry uses DirectX clockwise winding against
  OpenGL's counter-clockwise front faces, inverting normal Z. Light direction is now a configurable
  `uLightDir` uniform; the harvest tool sets its Z sign per client build.
- **Tone mapping always applied** — the Reinhard tone-map (exposure=20) now applies at every time of
  day, not just noon. Without it the raw linear response was ~2.79× darker than authored minimaps.
- `--time-hours` accepts any time of day (`HHmm`, `HH:mm`, or decimal); default stays 12:00. The sun
  is frozen at that value — synthetic output never advances a live clock, and the manifest records
  `timeOfDayMode=frozen`. Map LIT and Light DBC profiles belong to the viewer and are deliberately
  never applied to minimap generation.
- Analytic cast shadows are an **addition the client never had** and default OFF for the Alpha era;
  `--cast-shadows` / `--no-cast-shadows` set them explicitly. Normal RGB output omits the client's
  baked MCSH map, which is a separate signal — `--bake-mcsh` is an exceptional-history preview only.
- New: `--authored-reference` emits real-client side-by-side comparisons, `--match-time` infers each
  tile's best-matching hour from the authored minimap's shading, `--tile-list "x,y;x,y"` bounds a
  set, `--detail` uses mip-filtered real BLP texels for 1024+ super-resolution targets, and
  `--score` scores output against the authored minimap.
- `--include-wmos` renders placed WMO geometry (experimental, needs a headless GL context,
  default off).
- New sibling commands: `sun-diagnostic` lights a synthetic hill at 06:00–18:00 with compass-marked
  PNGs and no client, to separate a wrong lighting model from wrongly-oriented terrain data;
  `control-corpus` writes a deterministic fully-supervised terrain control corpus.
- **Liquid height gating** — liquid pixels are skipped where the liquid surface sits below terrain at
  the same cell. WoW liquid is a plane system, not painted coverage. Applies to WL*, MCLQ, and MH2O.
- Pure-C# DXT1 codec, encoding survey, and lighting baseline; BLP2 header byte offsets corrected.

## PM4 (Specs 128–131, PM4 restoration epic)

- **Coordinate frames solved and confirmed**: MSVT is ADT placement space (origin − value, fields
  opposite to MDDF). The bug was the per-object MPRL fitter, not the axis convention —
  `Pm4PlacementMath` was right. All tiles now align.
- **Connective geometry decoded**: MSPV/MSPI is a vertical planar quad mesh — the wall geometry that
  seals PM4's negative volume against MSUR floors. Rendered in the viewer with a discoverable toggle.
- **Object identity**: a keyed CK24 is one placed WMO; CK24 0 is the per-tile remainder that splits
  into per-doodad components. `MSLK.GroupObjectId` is the per-doodad identity.
- PM4 scene graph tree view restored; bounds audit explains why tile 0_0 is a dumping ground; MPRR
  full-domain sweep and sentinel run structure recorded.
- Fixed the PM4 tab showing the wrong panel and walls drawing nothing in wireframe.

## Datasets, harvest, and terrain ML (Specs 125–141)

- **v60 unified dataset**: streams directly from the C# harvest tool into Zarr with no intermediates.
  Recursive client discovery, multi-threaded harvest, per-build stores, `--resume`, `--skip-builds`,
  per-tile timeouts, and on-the-fly dedup in a single pass.
- Single-writer thread for the Zarr store to avoid Windows file-lock errors; unique arrays written
  directly via resize rather than append.
- `terrain_shadow_256` emitted from the harvest pipeline; sidecar signals (`signal_class`,
  `surviving_height_levels`) computed in-store.
- v60 clean-signal lane: model contract, trainer contract, loss profiles, corpus builder, visual
  review, training CLI, and recorded gate results (promotion, complete-family, reflect-padding).
- Provenance-aware dataset and real tile selection; full v50.1 real-terrain bridge with map holdout
  (Kalimdor train, Azeroth validation).
- Three-tier terrain brush signature classification; weak-signal tile archaeology pipeline; hole
  masks, signal-mismatch detection, and textured composites.
- Sparse MCCV preservation fix for 3.x–4.x: a short MCNR declaration caused the padded subchunk walk
  to skip a following 580-byte MCCV payload.
- Constitution v2.0.0 retires the V14 residual-model-chain prohibition.

## Proof boundary and known issues

This project separates *implemented* from *proven*. The following are implemented with focused tests
and clean builds, but real-client visual, audible, and FPS proof is user-owned and still open:

- Portal visibility FPS/submission comparison against the native 0.5.3 renderer.
- Audible playback proof for MCSE emitters, water/environment triggers, and ZoneMusic.
- MDX shader compilation and translucent/reflective model appearance across client eras.
- Compact-window reachability for the consolidated sidebar IA.
- Early M2 rendering remains **provisional for client builds 1.0.0 through 3.0.0**. The profiled
  embedded-native route is implemented, but material/mesh visibility needs real-client proof. Builds
  0.11/0.12 use the older MDX type; 3.3.5/4.0 remain the strongest late-client paths.
- `--include-wmos` minimap compositing is experimental and can fail or produce incorrect output.
- Synthesized minimaps render Lambert hillshading only by default. Analytic cast shadows
  (`--cast-shadows`) are a deliberate addition the original client never had, not a fidelity
  improvement, and MCSH is a separate terrain-side static signal rather than per-object shadows.
- No v60 authored real-data corpus has passed the albedo gate; nothing is training.
- Native file dialogs are Windows-only. On Linux/macOS, load content via `--game-path`, `--build`,
  and `--world`.
